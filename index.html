We build cameras that see beyond the image.

The camera is no longer only a lens and a sensor. It is an integrated system of optics, computation and intelligence—designed together to capture more of the world’s optical information.

01 / CAMERA AS AN INFORMATION MACHINE

Our question

Why haven’t cameras improved as much as other information technologies?

Once computation becomes part of the camera, the recovered image no longer has to be the physical image formed by a lens. Measurements across time, aperture, spectrum and illumination can be combined to estimate the scene.

We co-design optical architecture, electronic focal planes and algorithms to push photography toward the physical limits of resolution, speed and spectral diversity.

Research directions

Capture more.
Infer better.

01

Gigapixel camera arrays

Compact, scalable optical architectures for high-resolution, wide-field and high-frame-rate capture.

02

Computational imaging

Joint design of measurement and reconstruction for cameras that estimate, rather than simply record.

03

Holographic imaging

Physics-driven reconstruction for high-throughput, three-dimensional sensing and microscopy.

04

Spectral systems

Compressive measurement architectures that recover rich spectral and spatial information efficiently.

05

AI for cameras

Algorithms that control capture, interpret extreme data rates and synthesize new viewpoints.
